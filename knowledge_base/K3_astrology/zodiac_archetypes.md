# Astrology: Zodiac Archetypes & Emotional Patterns

## When to retrieve
User mentions their zodiac sign, asks about astrological influences, discusses compatibility, or references Mercury retrograde / eclipses / planetary transits.

## Philosophical framing
Astrology in 留白 is used as a METAPHORICAL LANGUAGE for self-reflection — a mirror, not a map. We neither claim it's scientifically proven nor dismiss it. It provides a rich symbolic vocabulary that helps people articulate feelings they can't otherwise name.

## The Big Three
- **Sun sign**: Core identity, conscious self
- **Moon sign**: Emotional inner world, what you need to feel safe
- **Rising sign**: How you appear to others, your social mask

**In conversation**: "Your Sun might be a logical Capricorn, but if your Moon is in Cancer, your emotional needs are very different from what you show the world. That gap can feel exhausting."

## Planetary transits as emotional context
When users mention feeling "off" without clear cause, planetary transits can provide a normalizing narrative: "A lot of people are feeling unsettled right now. Sometimes it helps to know you're not the only one." This is not about prediction — it's about reducing isolation.

## Application rules
1. Only introduce astrology if the user shows interest first.
2. Always frame as "one lens among many" — never as deterministic truth.
3. Use astrological language to VALIDATE, not to PREDICT or LIMIT.
4. "As a Scorpio, you might recognize this pattern..." not "Because you're a Scorpio, you will..."

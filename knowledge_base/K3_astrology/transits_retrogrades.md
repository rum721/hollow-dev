# Astrology: Transits & Retrogrades

## When to retrieve
User mentions feeling "off" without clear cause, asks about Mercury retrograde, Saturn return, eclipse season, or references specific planetary movements.

## Key life transits

### Saturn Return (ages ~29-30, ~58-59)
Saturn takes 29.5 years to orbit the Sun. When it returns to its birth position, it triggers a "life audit" — questioning career, relationships, identity. Often accompanied by major life changes, breakdowns, or breakthroughs.

**In conversation**: "You're around 29-30? That's a significant transition period. A lot of people find themselves questioning everything at this age — it's not a crisis, it's a recalibration."

### Pluto transits
Slow, deep transformation. Pluto conjunct a natal planet can trigger years of intense change — death and rebirth of an identity, relationship, or career. The process is painful but ultimately transformative.

**In conversation**: "What you're describing sounds like something very deep is shifting — not just the surface situation, but something fundamental about who you are. That kind of transformation takes time."

### Eclipse seasons
Eclipses (every ~6 months) are symbolically associated with sudden revelations, endings, and beginnings. They activate the nodal axis — karma, destiny, life direction.

**In conversation**: Use only as normalizing context: "Eclipse season tends to stir things up for a lot of people. If things feel chaotic right now, you're not alone."

## Retrograde periods

| Planet | Frequency | Duration | Symbolic meaning |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Mercury | 3-4x/year | ~3 weeks | Communication review, misunderstandings, revisiting past |
| Venus | Every 18 months | ~6 weeks | Relationship reassessment, old lovers returning |
| Mars | Every 2 years | ~2.5 months | Energy/motivation dip, anger review |
| Saturn | Annual | ~4.5 months | Restructuring, karmic lessons |
| Jupiter | Annual | ~4 months | Reassessing growth and beliefs |

### Retrograde as metaphor
Retrogrades are not about "things going wrong." They're about REVIEW — going back over what was missed. Mercury retrograde isn't "don't sign contracts" — it's "re-read the fine print of your life."

**In conversation**: "Mercury retrograde gets a bad reputation, but it's actually a good time to pause and review. What have you been moving too fast to notice?"

## Application rules
1. Use transits to NORMALIZE, not to PREDICT doom.
2. Frame as "many people experience..." not "the planets are causing..."
3. Transit knowledge is most useful when the user already believes in astrology — it meets them where they are.
4. Always pair astrological framing with practical grounding: "And regardless of what the stars say, what feels true for you right now?"

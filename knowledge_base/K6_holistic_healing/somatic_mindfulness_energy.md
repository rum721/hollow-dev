# Holistic Healing: Somatic, Mindfulness, Energy & Tarot

## When to retrieve
User discusses body sensations, meditation, energy work, chakras, tarot, breathwork, or asks for practical self-regulation techniques.

## Somatic Experiencing (Peter Levine)

### Core concept
Trauma is stored in the body, not just the mind. The body keeps the score. Healing requires completing the body's interrupted stress responses — the fight/flight/freeze that never resolved.

### Polyvagal Theory (Stephen Porges) — Three states

| State | Nervous system | Feels like | Body signs | What the user needs |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Ventral Vagal | Social engagement | Safe, connected, curious | Relaxed face, open posture, steady voice | Deeper exploration is possible |
| Sympathetic | Fight or flight | Anxious, angry, restless | Tight chest, racing heart, clenched jaw | Co-regulation: slow voice, grounding |
| Dorsal Vagal | Freeze/shutdown | Numb, empty, disconnected | Flat voice, collapsed posture, glazed eyes | Gentle warmth, small sensory prompts |

### Grounding techniques for conversation

**5-4-3-2-1 Sensory grounding**: "Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste."

**Bilateral stimulation**: "Try crossing your arms and alternately tapping your shoulders — left, right, left, right — slowly, for about 30 seconds."

**Orienting**: "Look around the room slowly. Let your eyes land on something that feels pleasant or neutral. Stay with it for a moment."

**In conversation**: "Before we go deeper into this, I want to check — how does your body feel right now? Is there tension anywhere?"

## Mindfulness & Meditation

### Core practices

**Breath awareness**: The simplest and most powerful. "Follow your breath — in through the nose, out through the mouth. Don't change it, just notice it."

**Body scan**: Systematic attention from head to toe. Useful for people who are disconnected from their body.

**Loving-kindness (Metta)**: "May I be safe. May I be healthy. May I be happy. May I live with ease." Then extend to others. Powerful for self-critical users.

**RAIN technique (Tara Brach)**:
- **R**ecognize: What is happening right now?
- **A**llow: Let it be here without fighting it.
- **I**nvestigate: Where do I feel this in my body? What does this feeling need?
- **N**urture: Offer yourself compassion. What would you say to a friend feeling this?

**In conversation**: "Let's try something. Take a slow breath in... and out... Now ask yourself: 'What am I feeling right now?' Not what you think you should feel — what's actually there?"

## Chakra System — Energy as metaphor

| Chakra | Location | Theme | Blocked feels like | Opening question |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Root (红) | Base of spine | Safety, survival | Anxiety, financial fear, ungrounded | "Do you feel safe in your life right now?" |
| Sacral (橙) | Lower abdomen | Pleasure, creativity, emotion | Guilt, creative block, emotional numbness | "When was the last time you did something purely for pleasure?" |
| Solar Plexus (黄) | Stomach | Power, identity, will | Shame, powerlessness, control issues | "Where in your life do you feel most in control? Least?" |

| Throat (蓝) | Throat | Expression, truth | Suppressed voice, fear of speaking up | "What are you not saying that needs to be said?" |

**In conversation**: Use as metaphorical language only. "It sounds like there's something stuck in your chest — a heaviness around the heart. What's there?" — not "Your heart chakra is blocked."

## Tarot — Archetypal storytelling

### How to use in 留白
Tarot is NOT fortune-telling. It's a projective tool — like a Rorschach test with beautiful imagery. The cards provide a symbolic vocabulary for the user to project their own feelings onto.

### Major Arcana as life themes
- **The Fool**: New beginnings, leap of faith
- **The Tower**: Sudden upheaval, necessary destruction
- **The Hermit**: Solitude, inner wisdom, withdrawal
- **Death**: Transformation, ending one chapter
- **The Star**: Hope after crisis, healing
- **The Wheel of Fortune**: Cycles, impermanence

**In conversation**: "What you're going through reminds me of The Tower in tarot — everything you built suddenly crumbling. But The Tower isn't about destruction for its own sake. It clears away what was unstable so something more authentic can be built."

### Application rules
1. Only use tarot imagery if the user is receptive to metaphorical/spiritual language.
2. Frame as storytelling, not prediction: "This archetype might resonate with where you are..."
3. The power is in the user's interpretation, not the card's "meaning."

## Universal application rules for all holistic modalities
1. Body-based techniques are offered as INVITATIONS, never commands: "Would you like to try..." not "You should..."
2. Always check: "How did that feel?" after any guided exercise.
3. These tools complement, never replace, professional care. If someone is in crisis, ground them first, then provide crisis resources.
4. Respect skepticism. If a user says "I don't believe in chakras," pivot immediately: "That's totally fine. Let me ask it differently — where in your body do you feel the most tension?"

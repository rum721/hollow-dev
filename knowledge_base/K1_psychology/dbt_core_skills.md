# DBT: Core Skills

## When to retrieve
User is experiencing intense emotions, interpersonal conflict, urge to act impulsively, or difficulty tolerating distress.

## Core philosophy
Dialectical Behavior Therapy (Marsha Linehan) holds two truths simultaneously: "I can accept myself as I am AND I can work to change." This dialectic — acceptance + change — is the foundation.

## Four skill modules

### 1. Mindfulness — "What" and "How" skills
Observe (notice without judging), Describe (put words to experience), Participate (fully engage in the moment). Practice non-judgmentally, one-mindfully, effectively.

**In conversation**: "Can you pause for a moment and just notice what you're feeling right now — without trying to change it?"

### 2. Distress Tolerance — Surviving crisis without making it worse
TIPP: Temperature (cold water on face), Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Progressive muscle relaxation.
ACCEPTS: Activities, Contributing, Comparisons, Emotions (opposite), Pushing away, Thoughts, Sensations.
Radical Acceptance: "This is what happened. I cannot change the past. I can only choose what I do next."

**In conversation**: When user is in acute distress — "Before we talk about this more, can we try something? Hold something cold — ice, a cold glass. Focus on that sensation for 30 seconds."

### 3. Emotion Regulation — Understanding and modulating emotions
Every emotion has a function (fear protects, anger defends boundaries, sadness signals loss). Emotions are like waves — they rise, peak, and fall. Opposite Action: when the emotion doesn't fit the facts, do the opposite of what the emotion urges.

**In conversation**: "That anger makes sense — it's protecting something important to you. What is it protecting?"

### 4. Interpersonal Effectiveness — DEAR MAN
Describe the situation, Express feelings, Assert needs, Reinforce the other person, stay Mindful, Appear confident, Negotiate. Use when user struggles to express needs in relationships.

**In conversation**: "What would you want to say to them if you knew they wouldn't react badly?"

# Eastern Metaphysics: BaZi, ZiWei DuShu & Numerology

## When to retrieve
User mentions 八字/BaZi, 紫微斗数/ZiWei, 命理, 五行, 生辰, life path number, or discusses fate/destiny from an Eastern philosophical perspective.

## Philosophical framing
Eastern metaphysical systems view life as the interplay of cosmic patterns and personal choice. In 留白, these systems serve as REFLECTIVE FRAMEWORKS — helping users understand their tendencies, not as deterministic predictions. "命" (fate) provides the terrain; "运" (luck/timing) provides the weather; but YOU choose how to walk the path.

## 八字 / BaZi (Four Pillars of Destiny)

### Core concept
Based on birth year, month, day, and hour — each mapped to a Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch, producing eight characters (八字). These reveal the balance of Five Elements (五行) in a person's constitution.

### Five Elements as emotional archetypes

| Element | Energy | Emotional tendency | Imbalance manifests as | Balancing approach |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 木 Wood | Growth, expansion | Ambition, vision, anger | Frustration, rigidity, liver issues | Flexibility, creative expression |
| 火 Fire | Illumination, passion | Joy, charisma, excitement | Anxiety, mania, scattered energy | Grounding, routine, stillness |
| 土 Earth | Stability, nurture | Caring, reliability, worry | Overthinking, codependency, stagnation | Movement, boundaries, letting go |
| 金 Metal | Precision, boundaries | Discipline, grief, clarity | Perfectionism, isolation, melancholy | Connection, softening, acceptance |
| 水 Water | Flow, depth | Wisdom, fear, adaptability | Paralysis, escapism, depression | Structure, courage, action |

### Practical application in conversation
**In conversation (Chinese)**: "你提到总是容易焦虑、想法很多停不下来——从五行的角度看，这可能是火气偏旺的表现。火的人天生热情有感染力，但需要找到让自己'降温'的方式。你平时有什么能让你安静下来的事情吗？"

**In conversation (English)**: "The restlessness you describe — always needing to move, start new things — that's a very 'Fire' quality in Chinese philosophy. It's a gift for leadership and inspiration, but it needs grounding. What helps you slow down?"

## 紫微斗数 / ZiWei DuShu (Purple Star Astrology)

### Core concept
The most sophisticated Chinese astrological system. Maps 14 major stars and 100+ minor stars across 12 life palaces (宫位), creating a detailed life chart.

### 12 Palaces as life dimensions

| Palace | Domain | Emotional relevance |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 命宫 Life | Core personality | Self-identity and self-worth |
| 兄弟宫 Siblings | Peer relationships | Friendship patterns, collaboration |
| 夫妻宫 Spouse | Romantic relationships | Love patterns, partnership dynamics |
| 子女宫 Children | Creativity, offspring | Creative expression, nurturing |
| 财帛宫 Wealth | Money relationship | Financial anxiety, abundance mindset |
| 疾厄宫 Health | Physical/mental health | Stress patterns, vulnerability |
| 迁移宫 Travel | External world | Adaptability, comfort zone |
| 交友宫 Friends | Social network | Trust, social belonging |
| 事业宫 Career | Professional life | Purpose, ambition, burnout |
| 田宅宫 Property | Home, security | Rootedness, stability needs |
| 福德宫 Fortune | Inner happiness | Emotional baseline, contentment |
| 父母宫 Parents | Authority figures | Family patterns, inherited beliefs |

**In conversation**: "你说事业上很成功但内心总觉得空——在紫微斗数里，事业宫和福德宫是两回事。事业宫管外在成就，福德宫管内在满足。两者不一定同步。"

## Universal application rules
1. Only introduce Eastern metaphysics when the user shows cultural affinity or explicit interest.
2. Always frame as "one perspective" — never as fate.
3. The most powerful use is VALIDATION: "This pattern you're experiencing makes sense within this framework."
4. Pair metaphysical insight with practical action: "And knowing this about yourself, what might you do differently?"
5. In Chinese conversations, these frameworks feel natural. In English, introduce more carefully and check receptivity first.
